
# B2B Lead Management Optimization | Business Analyst Case Study

An end-to-end Business Analyst project focused on identifying process inefficiencies in B2B lead management, performing gap analysis, documenting business requirements, and proposing process improvements to reduce lead response and follow-up delays.

## Project Overview

B2B sales teams often manage leads across multiple channels, spreadsheets, emails, and CRM platforms. Without standardized processes, this can result in delayed responses, inconsistent lead qualification, missed follow-ups, incomplete CRM records, and inefficient sales handoffs.

This project examines a simulated B2B lead management process and proposes a structured, CRM-driven approach to improve operational efficiency, data quality, and lead handling.

**Project Type:** Business Analysis | Process Optimization | Operational Analytics  
**Domain:** B2B Sales / Business Development  
**Dataset:** 500 Synthetic B2B Lead Records  
**Tools:** Microsoft Excel, Draw.io, Microsoft Word, GitHub

## Business Problem

The existing lead management workflow is assumed to involve manual activities across different stages:

- Lead capture from multiple sources.
- Manual lead assignment and qualification.
- Delayed initial outreach.
- Inconsistent follow-up tracking.
- Incomplete CRM data.
- Delayed handoff of qualified leads to the sales team.

The objective is to identify these potential process gaps and recommend improvements through structured business analysis.

## Project Objectives

- Understand and document the existing lead management process.
- Analyze lead data to identify operational patterns and potential bottlenecks.
- Develop As-Is and To-Be process maps.
- Perform gap analysis and identify potential root causes.
- Document business requirements and improvement opportunities.
- Estimate the potential business impact of the proposed improvements.

## Project Methodology

### 1. Business Understanding
- Defined the business scenario and problem statement.
- Established project objectives, scope, and stakeholders.
- Identified the lead management stages.

### 2. Dataset Preparation and Analysis
- Created a synthetic dataset containing 500 B2B lead records.
- Used Excel to perform data checks and calculations.
- Analyzed lead response, qualification, follow-up, and handoff-related information.
- Examined potential data quality issues and operational delays.

### 3. Process Mapping
- Developed an As-Is process map to document the existing workflow.
- Identified potential manual activities and process bottlenecks.
- Designed a To-Be process map incorporating standardized workflows and CRM-based tracking.
- Created a Fishbone diagram to organize potential root causes.

### 4. Business Requirements Documentation
- Prepared a Business Requirements Document (BRD).
- Documented business objectives, scope, requirements, and proposed improvements.

### 5. Gap Analysis
- Compared the current process with the desired future state.
- Identified process gaps and potential root causes.
- Prioritized improvement opportunities.

### 6. Improvement Recommendations
- Recommend centralized lead tracking through CRM.
- Introduce standardized lead qualification criteria.
- Establish response-time and follow-up SLAs.
- Automate follow-up reminders and task assignments.
- Improve CRM data completeness through mandatory-field validation.
- Standardize qualified-lead handoff.

### 7. Business Impact Assessment
- Estimate potential reductions in manual processing time.
- Evaluate improvements in response-time compliance.
- Assess expected improvements in CRM data quality.
- Identify operational efficiency opportunities.

## Key Business KPIs

The following KPIs are proposed for evaluating process performance.

| KPI | Baseline | Proposed Target |
|---|---:|---:|
| Average Lead Response Time | 24 Hours | 4 Hours |
| Follow-up SLA Compliance | 60% | 90% |
| CRM Data Completeness | 70% | 95% |
| Qualified Lead Handoff Time | 12 Hours | 3 Hours |
| Manual Processing Time per Lead | 15 Minutes | 7 Minutes |

**Important:** These are illustrative scenario baselines and proposed targets, not verified company performance results. They should be validated against the dataset and, ideally, a real-world pilot before being presented as achieved improvements.
